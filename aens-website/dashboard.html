Simulation notes: both aircraft use the AENS-R1 reference model from the technology page, 25 kg, glide ratio 30:1, 0.6 m/s sink, 400 Wh pack, 226 W to hold altitude on motor, 65% powertrain efficiency. Thermals are placed randomly each reset with strengths of 1.5 to 3 m/s depending on the weather setting, and drift with the wind. Aircraft land, in the sim, at a 15% battery reserve. This is a visualization of the product's behavior, not flight test data.
